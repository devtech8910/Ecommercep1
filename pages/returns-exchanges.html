Returns & Exchanges

Your satisfaction is our priority. If an item doesn't fit perfectly or isn't exactly what you expected, we offer a hassle-free 30-day return and exchange policy.

Conditions for Returns

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition.
  • All original tags and packaging must be intact.
  • Intimate wear and accessories (like earrings) are non-returnable for hygiene reasons.

How to Exchange

To exchange an item for a different size or color, please initiate a return request from your Account Dashboard and place a new order for the desired item.
