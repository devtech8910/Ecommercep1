# Fashion Company

## Client Visual Showcase

Fashion Company is a fashion e-commerce storefront with curated product collections, catalog browsing, and detailed product pages. The screens below are a visual walkthrough captured from the project; this page is read-only and does not collect information, sign users in, or place orders.

### Browse the catalog

Search and explore products across men's wear, women's wear, kids' fashion, and accessories.

### View a product

Product details bring together product photography, available options, delivery information, and shopping actions.

The production storefront is temporarily not recommended for customer transactions while its Google Safe Browsing review is pending. This showcase is provided so the design and product experience can still be reviewed in the meantime.
